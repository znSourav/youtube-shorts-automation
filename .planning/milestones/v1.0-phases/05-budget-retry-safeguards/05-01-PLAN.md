**Two deliberate corrections to research, both grounded in data read during planning:**

1. **The historical migration.** Research recommended importing all 40 ledger entries. The database already holds 14 of them as `GenerationRecord` rows ($2.1350) from Phase 3's dual-write. Importing all 40 would record $7.207 of spend and silently take $2.135 of real headroom from her. Plan 05-02 imports only the 26 unpaired entries ($2.9370), reaching exactly $5.0720.
2. **The month-allocation upsert.** Research's snippet used an empty update clause. That means a month first touched before the requester edits his figure keeps a stale allocation for the whole month — an over-allocation of real money that defeats BUDGET-02. Plan 05-01 sets the current month's allocation to the configured figure on every check, leaving past months frozen; the failure direction becomes fail-closed.

Also flagged during planning and handled: with the deferred spend flush, a story creation that is blocked, unparseable, invalid, or unsaveable would have left its dispatched, billed calls unrecorded once the durable table became authoritative. Plan 05-03 moves the write to the dispatch boundary to close it.

Every exported function takes trailing optional collaborators in this order: `client: PrismaClient = prisma`, `env: Record<string, string | undefined> = process.env`, `now: Date = new Date()` — only as many as it actually uses. `ensureCurrentMonthAllocation` resolves the month key and the configured figure and performs a single `client.budgetPeriod.upsert` keyed on `{ month }`.
