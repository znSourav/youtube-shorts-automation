<objective>
Make the generated assets and their real costs durable: every scene image and video records its saved path and status against its own database row, and every paid call — story, uniqueness comparison, image, and video — leaves a record carrying its estimated cost. Then close the loop by restoring the last story's scenes and statuses when the browser is reopened.

Purpose: plan 03-01 made a story durable and plan 03-02 made that history mean something; without this plan the scenes and the money spent on them still evaporate on restart, which is exactly what PERSIST-01, IMAGE-01, IMAGE-03, and VIDEO-03 forbid. Task 1 delivers the durability; Task 2 delivers the restore the wife actually experiences.
Output: A generation-record repository, dual-write wiring at both paid asset call sites, a browser-safe restore path, and offline integration coverage for all of it.
</objective>
