<display_decision>
05-RESEARCH.md Open Question #2 left the primary figure's semantics to this step, with a recommendation this plan adopts: **the headline figure is the rollover-inclusive one** — total allocated across every month minus total spent across every month — because that is precisely the number `checkBudget` compares against, so the figure she reads is the figure that decides whether her next click is refused. Showing this month's own allocation as the headline would let her read a comfortable number and still be refused, which is the one failure mode a spend indicator exists to prevent. This month's own allocation and its own by-type spend appear in the expanded detail, which is also where BUDGET-03's literal "month-to-date spend broken down by generation type against the configured limit" lands.

At the time this ships the two are identical — one month exists, at $15.00, with $5.0720 spent — so the headline reads $9.93 left of $15.00. They diverge from month two onward, which is exactly when the distinction starts mattering.
</display_decision>
