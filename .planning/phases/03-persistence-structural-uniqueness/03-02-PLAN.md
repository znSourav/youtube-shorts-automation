<objective>
Insert the structural-uniqueness gate between story generation and story review, so no story reaches the wife if it is a structural reskin of one she already made — and so two genuinely different stories that merely share generic surface elements are not falsely rejected.

Purpose: this is the phase's product substance. Plan 03-01 proved a story can be persisted and read back; this plan makes that persisted history mean something. It is built as three vertical slices: the deterministic gate wired end to end first (zero LLM cost, the common path), then the targeted LLM tie-breaker for the borderline case, then the plain-language surfacing the wife actually experiences.
Output: A working collision detector, a bounded regeneration loop, a ceiling-gated tie-breaker, and the D-03/D-04 wording she sees.
</objective>
