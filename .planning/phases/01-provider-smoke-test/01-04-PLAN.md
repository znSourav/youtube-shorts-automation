<objective>
Run D-01's second, representative generation — a child-protagonist scene in the "Soft hand-painted 2D"
style — and establish empirically what the two providers actually charge.

Purpose: this entire channel is animated stories about child protagonists. Whether Gemini's and Veo's
safety filters false-positive on wholesome children's-story content is a real, product-specific risk, and
D-01 exists to catch it in Phase 1 rather than after Phases 2-4 are built on the untested assumption that
it works. If the probe is blocked, that is a critical, reportable finding about provider viability — not a
bug to retry past quietly. The cost reconciliation closes ROADMAP SC-3 honestly: RESEARCH.md's price
figures are page-scraped assumptions (Assumption A4, Open Question 2), and Phase 5's budget system should
inherit measured numbers.

Output: a second image and clip on disk, a classified probe outcome, and a corrected price table.

Expected spend for this plan: one image call at roughly $0.067 plus one 8-second 720p clip at $0.40 =
**$0.467**, plus at most one retry. Phase total lands near $0.73 of the $3.00 ceiling.
