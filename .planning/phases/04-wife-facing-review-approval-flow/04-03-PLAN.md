<objective>
Deliver D-04 and D-05: one "Generate All Videos" action that kicks off every approved scene's video job, and a genuinely new fourth screen purpose-built for watching those jobs progress, retrying a single failed scene, and reaching the finished episode.

Purpose: this is where VIDEO-02 and VIDEO-04 land — the only part of the wife-facing flow where N independent, minutes-long, money-spending jobs run at once. It is also where 04-RESEARCH.md's Pitfall 1 bites hardest: two new callers of the paid video dispatch appear at the same time, and both must be structurally unable to skip the approval gate and the retry cap that plan 04-01 installed.

Output: a pure, injectable batch orchestrator; three new Server Actions; a new VideoStatusScreen component with DB-polled per-scene status; a "capped" state on SceneVideo; and a GENERATING status that makes an in-flight job visible instead of indistinguishable from never-started.
</objective>
