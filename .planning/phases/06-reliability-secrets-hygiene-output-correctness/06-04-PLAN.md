<objective>
Close D-05: a file that this app failed to save correctly costs her a real generation but never one of her limited creative attempts — the exemption is a persisted, server-read, one-shot fact that survives a page reload, and it can never weaken the approval gate or the budget gate it sits beside.

Purpose: plan 06-03 made a corrupted save detectable and durably recorded. This plan makes that record actually mean something: a cap bypass in the pure decision layer, spent at the dispatch boundary, for both the video and the image path.

Output: three extended decision functions with exhaustive unit coverage, and three dispatch sites that spend the exemption instead of charging an attempt.

Deliberate asymmetry, recorded so it is not later "fixed": a technical generation failure (timeout, malformed response, content block) still consumes an attempt per D-02, because the cap's job is to stop an accidental click-loop from burning money and that applies regardless of cause. A local save-integrity failure does not consume one per D-05, because the provider did its job and the bug was this app's own write step. These are genuinely different categories, not an inconsistency.

Budget note: this plan dispatches ZERO paid provider calls.
</objective>
