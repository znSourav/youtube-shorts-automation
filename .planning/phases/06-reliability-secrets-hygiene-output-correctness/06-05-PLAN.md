<objective>
Retire the stuck-generation detector's browser-only clock in favour of the server-anchored timestamp plan 06-03 added, make the corrupted-save exemption visible where she actually encounters it, and close the phase with a human confirmation of all four success criteria against the running app.

Purpose: a scene that has genuinely been in flight for half an hour currently shows a fresh twelve-minute countdown after every page reload, silently deferring the recovery affordance — a real defect carried forward from Phase 4's sixth review pass. And a scene granted a free retry by plan 06-04 would still render as a capped dead end unless the status action stops reporting it as capped. Both are the last places where the phase's server-side guarantees have not yet reached her screen.

Output: a shared threshold constant, a server-computed stuck and save-corrupted signal on the status row, a client that consumes both instead of guessing, and a phase-closing verification checkpoint.

Budget note: this plan is designed to dispatch ZERO paid provider calls, including its checkpoint.
</objective>
